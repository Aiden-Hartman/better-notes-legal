Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Better Notes helps field sales representatives record customer visits, process business cards and voice notes, identify businesses, and prepare updates to a connected HubSpot CRM. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, who helps process it, and how to control it.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: email, account identifiers, authentication and linked-identity information, and the identity of a connected HubSpot user and portal.
  • Notes and CRM information: text notes, business and contact details (such as names, titles, emails and phone numbers), meetings, follow-up tasks, proposed CRM changes, and related records.
  • Business cards and voice notes: photos, recorded audio, transcripts, extracted information, and related file metadata when you use these features.
  • Location and movement: precise location, motion and detected visits, including background information when you grant the relevant iOS permissions, to suggest nearby businesses and recent stops.
  • Operational information: app version, device and installation signals, feature activity, diagnostic events, processing status, error information, and data needed to resolve locations or synchronize notes.

Information about other people may appear in business cards, notes or a connected company's CRM. Use Better Notes only with information you are authorized to handle.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts; capture, enrich, save and synchronize notes; recognize business-card and voice content; identify nearby places and visits; research public business contact information; prepare CRM updates for your review; prevent duplicate updates; diagnose errors; and operate and improve the app. We do not operate a third-party advertising network or use submitted note content for targeted advertising.

3. AI processing and your choices

Better Notes asks for permission before sending card images, voice recordings or transcripts, business research inputs, or CRM note text to third-party AI processing. The disclosures identify the applicable processing purpose and providers. You can decline a request or withdraw previously saved AI permissions from Settings; relevant AI functions will not run without permission. Withdrawing permission stops future processing but does not retroactively erase information already processed.

For AI processing, Better Notes uses the OpenAI API. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train models by default unless the API customer opts in. Standard abuse-monitoring logs may be retained up to 30 days, subject to exceptions; certain API endpoints, including Responses, can also retain application state. Provider handling depends on the specific API and account settings.

4. Location and movement

With your authorization, Better Notes collects current and background device location and movement evidence to suggest recent stops and nearby businesses. It uses Foursquare Movement and Foursquare Places. The Foursquare Movement SDK may transmit device coordinates, visit times, installation/device identifiers, and available sensor or network signals to Foursquare to recognize places. Foursquare's own data-processing terms also apply. You can restrict or turn off location permissions in iOS Settings and continue using manual capture where available.

5. Providers and sharing

We use providers only for relevant app functions:

  • Supabase: account authentication, encrypted session-related services, database, private capture-file storage and backend functions. The project's primary database, Auth and Storage are hosted in AWS US East (Northern Virginia); processing and operations may occur elsewhere.
  • OpenAI: business-card extraction, voice transcription, note interpretation and AI-assisted research.
  • Foursquare: movement/visit detection and place information.
  • Hunter and Serper, when enabled: business and professional-contact discovery using relevant names, websites, domains or search queries.
  • HubSpot: user-authorized CRM reads and writes. Proposed changes are shown for review before they are submitted.
  • Resend, through configured authentication email delivery: email addresses and authentication messages needed to sign in.
  • Expo, and Apple's operating system and distribution services: app version, platform and relevant technical information needed to deliver app builds and updates.

We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service, subject to applicable law. Service providers may process information in other countries under their own privacy notices, data-processing terms and safeguards.

6. Storage and retention

Notes, extracted information, account data, permissions, CRM plans and synced location evidence are stored in the Better Notes backend and, where needed, in account-specific files on your device. Better Notes does not currently apply a fixed automatic expiration period to most backend account records or stored captures; they remain until the account is deleted or otherwise removed. Temporary capture files may be deleted when processing finishes.

Raw local location/movement telemetry is scheduled to be pruned when the app next starts, keeping no more than seven days or ten sessions under the current implementation. This is a best-effort on-device limit and does not cover location evidence already synchronized to the backend.

Provider-side logs, backups and processed data follow the providers' applicable terms and retention practices, which may differ from Better Notes' account deletion timing. For example, Resend describes a standard 30-day email and log retention period for certain plans, and Supabase backup retention varies by plan. No universal third-party deletion deadline is promised.

7. Account deletion and CRM records

From Better Notes Settings, choose Delete Account and confirm to initiate deletion of your Better Notes account, owned notes, associated app data and private capture files. The app attempts to revoke linked Apple and HubSpot authorizations and clears local account-specific data. If authorization revocation cannot be completed automatically, it provides steps to revoke access with the provider. Certain information may remain in provider backups or records retained for legal or security obligations.

Important: deleting your Better Notes account does not delete records already sent to an employer's or organization's HubSpot portal. Those CRM records are governed by that organization's data policies and administrators. To request access, correction or deletion of company CRM data, contact the organization controlling that portal.

8. Security and organizational use

Better Notes uses authenticated accounts, restricted data access, private file storage, and encrypted storage for certain connected-account credentials. HubSpot operations use the permissions of the individually authorized salesperson. No online system can guarantee absolute security. If you use Better Notes for an employer, your organization's customer-data and device policies also apply.

9. Privacy rights, children and changes

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain information about your personal data. Contact [PUBLIC PRIVACY EMAIL] to make a privacy request. Better Notes is intended for working professionals and not designed for children under 13.
